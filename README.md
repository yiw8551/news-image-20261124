A deterministic Python program (Pillow only) that sums up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single 1600x3983 PNG. The front page carries 39 stories from 10 regions: three featured stories (tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz, Huawei convicted on 10 of 12 counts in Brooklyn, and the Dow closing above 50,000 as the AI rally fades) plus 36 compact numbered stories spanning the Middle East, the Russia-Ukraine war, Europe, East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America, North America and global news — from the Houthis striking Riyadh's tallest tower a second time and Senegal regaining its Africa Cup title in court, to the first foreign astronaut bound for China's Tiangong station, COP31 ending with no pact, and a record 5,258 heat deaths in England. The program uses no network or external files and produces byte-identical output on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `output/entry.png` next to the script.
